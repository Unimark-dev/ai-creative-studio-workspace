# Quality standards

The bar is work a premium agency would present. Look at every result yourself before showing it, and be the first critic: the user should not be the one to spot a warped label.

## What to aim for

Art direction with a clear idea. Photorealistic rendering when the concept calls for it, with sophisticated light, believable materials and textures, professional color grading, and refined balance.

## Pre-delivery check

**Product,** compared to the reference, zooming in on the label:
- Shape, proportions and color are correct
- Label text reads the same, including small print
- Logo on the product is unchanged
- No invented details, such as extra caps, seams or text
- It sits in the scene, with contact shadow and matching light

**Brand,** compared to the profile:
- Palette holds, and no off-brand color dominates
- Tone matches the stated personality
- No listed restriction is broken
- It looks like the same brand as earlier work in the campaign

**Craft:**
- One clear focal point and a visual idea
- Negative space is really empty where copy goes
- Nothing unexplained: stray objects, garbled background text, fake logos
- No anatomical errors if people appear
- No visible seams or repeated textures on extended areas

**Final,** after compositing:
- Exact requested dimensions
- Copy matches the approved text exactly
- Arabic joined and right-to-left, with diacritics and numerals correct
- Compose report shows no missing fonts, broken images or overflow
- Text is legible on its background, at the size people will see it

## What to avoid

Generic AI aesthetics, repetitive compositions, random decoration, excessive effects, inconsistent brand colors, distorted products, poor lighting, clutter.

## When something fails

- Fixable through layout: fix it without spending credits.
- Prompt-related: retry once with a specific correction, and say why.
- Label softening on an otherwise good image: paste the original product back, if the geometry and light allow.
- Still imperfect: show it, name the flaw plainly, and offer options. An honest "the small print on the label softened at this size" is more useful than a confident delivery someone has to catch later.

Automated checks can't judge everything. Tell the user to check small print and legal text themselves before anything is printed or launched, since a person with the actual product in hand is the final check on it.
